Pricing & fees

What you pay, what you win

Simple, transparent pricing — no hidden charges. Every order carries a flat 2% trading fee (minimum $0.01), and that's the only cost. Here's exactly how it works.

  1. 01

    Contracts are priced 1¢–99¢

    Every market is priced in cents. The price reflects the live likelihood of the outcome. Each contract settles at $1.00 if your side wins, or $0.00 if it doesn't.

  2. 02

    What you pay

    Your cost is the contract price × the number of contracts, plus the 2% trading fee. The full breakdown is shown in your order summary before you confirm.

  3. 03

    What you can win

    If your side settles in your favour, each contract pays out $1.00. Your potential profit is the payout less your cost and fees.

  4. 04

    Settlement

    Markets settle automatically the moment the live result is final, and winnings are credited to your balance right away.
