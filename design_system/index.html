How to Use It

Follow this order when implementing or reviewing a feature.

  1. 01
    Open the relevant Page.

    Review the implementation, its contract, required states, and open validation together.

  2. 02
    Use documented states as the coverage checklist.

    Every default, loading, empty, error, validation, and responsive state sits on its owning Page.

  3. 03
    Build with production primitives and patterns.

    Use only tokens listed here and reuse the closest documented component variant.

  4. 04
    Escalate unresolved behaviour.

    Do not turn a prototype alternative into production behaviour until its decision is resolved.
